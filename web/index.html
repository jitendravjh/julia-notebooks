A conjecture that was false

Keller asked in 1939 whether a polynomial map whose derivative is invertible everywhere must itself be invertible. It held for eighty-seven years and broke in July 2026. The counterexample is verified here exactly, then explained by a sheet that folds along a curve and covers the inside twice.
